# Stoma3D primary-source bibliography

Last checked for planning: **2026-07-21**. These links support design and competition
planning; they do not establish regulatory compliance, clinical validity, dataset
permission, or submission acceptance. Recheck each source and the live competition portal
against the frozen release.

## Product, safety, and privacy wording

- [FDA - How to Determine if Your Product Is a Medical Device](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/classify-your-medical-device/how-determine-if-your-product-medical-device)
  informs intended-use review and the decision to avoid diagnostic or disease-detection
  claims. It is guidance for review, not a determination about Stoma3D.
- [FTC - Complying with the Health Breach Notification Rule](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-ftcs-health-breach-notification-rule-0)
  informs health-app privacy and breach-response review. Linking it is not a compliance
  claim or legal advice.

## Competition rules and district administration

- [Official 2026 Congressional App Challenge rules PDF](https://www.congressionalappchallenge.us/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/2026-CAC-Rules.pdf)
  is the controlling planning source for the maximum four-person team, one-to-three-minute
  public video, AI/open-source disclosure, and the national October 26, 2026 noon EDT
  deadline. The live portal must still be confirmed.
- [NJ-06 announcement from Representative Frank Pallone](https://pallone.house.gov/media/press-releases/pallone-announces-coding-and-app-building-competition-nj-students-0)
  is the district source. Its October 26/28 language conflicts internally or with the
  national wording, so the team must obtain written clarification and keep the earlier
  internal October 23 deadline.

## Dataset and capture structure

- [SMART-OM: an annotated smartphone oral-cavity image dataset](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41597-026-06954-5)
  supports the canonical eight-region capture vocabulary and informs the dataset-risk
  discussion. Its imbalance and cross-sectional design do not validate longitudinal
  re-identification or a disease classifier, and the paper/download page alone is not a
  redistribution license.

## Mobile implementation

- [Expo SDK latest-version documentation](https://docs.expo.dev/versions/latest/)
  is the primary reference for the Expo modules used by the mobile working tree. Confirm
  the documentation against the exact `pnpm-lock.yaml` versions and physical development
  builds; documentation compatibility is not device-test evidence.
