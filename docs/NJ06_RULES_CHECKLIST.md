## App and intellectual-property package

- [ ] App is original student work and each student can explain the complete architecture.
- [ ] Locked source commit/build is runnable and matches the video.
- [ ] AI assistance is disclosed and is not the entire technical contribution.
- [ ] All open-source code, fonts, images, audio, 3D assets, datasets, and models have
      license/provenance rows and required attribution.
- [ ] No restricted medical data, secret, credential, or third-party personal information
      is included.
- [ ] Public wording follows `INTENDED_USE_AND_FORBIDDEN_CLAIMS.md`.

## Video and submission answers

- [ ] Public demonstration is one to three minutes; target locked cut is 2:45–2:55.
- [ ] Names, audience/problem, product workflow, technical implementation, privacy,
      limitations, AI assistance, and open-source use are included.
- [ ] Captions and readable screen recording are present; soundtrack/media rights are
      documented.
- [ ] Video URL opens without login in a private/incognito check.
- [ ] Submission answers and app title are consistent with the repository and video.
- [ ] Any required source/code link has the correct access setting and no sensitive history.
