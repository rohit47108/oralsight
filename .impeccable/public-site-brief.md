# OralSight public site direction

## Approved composition

Use `mocks/public-direction-b-guided-scan.png` as the public-site north star.
The user delegated the final design choice. Direction B was selected because its
numbered scan path explains the product immediately, keeps the phone workflow as
the focal point, and remains calm without resembling a generic software landing
page.

Use the information density of direction C only inside authenticated clinician
workspaces. Do not copy its fictional anatomy, dates, results, or controls.

## Direction contract

- Open with the real four-part story: consent, eight-region capture, observation
  review, and private sharing.
- Use an asymmetric editorial grid, porcelain ground, graphite text, mineral
  teal actions, and amber only for a meaningful attention state.
- Keep the public page imagery non-graphic. Product UI and the canonical region
  illustration carry the story; no patient faces, stock people, or fabricated
  medical output.
- Use direct language and one primary action. Avoid diagnostic, clinical-
  accuracy, compliance, and reassurance claims.
- Motion is limited to short state and navigation feedback and respects reduced
  motion.

The generated comp is not literal product specification. Its invented region
names, mock scan image, privacy statements, access language, and layouts must be
replaced by real contracts, real product states, tested responsive behavior, and
approved copy.
